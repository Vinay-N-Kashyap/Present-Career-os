Correct: Option B) Symmetric Deviation Threshold Flaw (Halting Legitimate Liquidations): The circuit breaker compared an instantaneous feed (Chainlink) against a lagging 30-minute TWAP! During rapid real-world crashes, a lagging TWAP naturally lags behind fast-moving spot prices by > 5%! The circuit breaker interpreted this legitimate market volatility as an attack, halting liquidations and generating massive Bad Debt! Circuit breakers must compare against external median feeds or price velocity, not lagging TWAPs. • Debrief: Comparing a fast-updating price oracle against a slow lagging TWAP during genuine high-volatility market crashes causes false-positive circuit breaker trips. The TWAP naturally trails the live price, creating a persistent divergence that pauses the protocol exactly when liquidations are most urgently needed to prevent bad debt.
