Fix: "It also limits market sell pressure during liquidations."] 🧩 Situational Diagnostic Puzzle: "During a massive crypto market crash, Ethereum network gas prices spike to 500 gwei. An under-collateralized borrow position has $100 of debt and $102 of collateral. Liquidators ignore the position, and the protocol accumulates $20 of Bad Debt. Why did liquidators refuse to liquidate this position?" Correct: Option B) Dust Position Gas Economic Unprofitability: Liquidating the position yields a 5% bonus ($5 profit). However, executing the liquidation transaction at 500 gwei gas costs $65 in Ethereum network transaction fees! The liquidator would lose $60 executing the liquidation! Because the position was too small ('dust position'), it was economically unviable to liquidate, causing the protocol to swallow the bad debt. &bull; Debrief: Dust positions are a major threat to DeFi solvency. When transaction gas fees exceed the liquidation bonus profit, rational liquidators will not liquidate underwater loans. Protocols mitigate this by enforcing minimum borrow sizes (e.g. minimum $5,000 borrow) or utilizing Layer 2 rollups with negligible gas costs.
