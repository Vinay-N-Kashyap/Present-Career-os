Understand the architectural leap of ERC-2535: instead of a proxy delegating to a single monolithic implementation contract, a 'Diamond' proxy delegates function calls to DOZENS of separate logic contracts called 'Facets'! When an incoming function call arrives, the Diamond extracts the 4-byte selector from calldata, looks up the specific Facet address in storage, and forwards the call via `delegatecall`. Master the Diamond Loupe interface (inspecting active facets and selectors) and `diamondCut()` (atomically adding, replacing, or removing facets).
