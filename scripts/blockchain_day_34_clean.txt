Understand how uncollateralized lending works without counterparty credit risk: borrow $100,000,000 with ZERO collateral, execute arbitrary smart contract logic (arbitrage, collateral swaps, liquidations), and repay the borrowed capital PLUS fee within the EXACT SAME ATOMIC TRANSACTION! If the borrower fails to repay in full at the end of the transaction, the EVM reverts the entire transaction, rewinding history as if the loan never occurred! Dissect the EIP-3156 Flash Loan Standard (`maxFlashLoan`, `flashFee`, `onFlashLoan`). Build an automated arbitrage bot capitalizing on cross-pool price discrepancies. Invariant: Atomic Repayment Invariant: A flash loan provider must verify at the end of the callback execution that pool balance after loan &ge; pool balance before loan + flashFee; if balance check fails, the transaction must revert atomically. ⚠️ Failure Mode &amp; tryIt Bug: Failure: Transferring flash loan capital without checking return data or assuming the receiver contract will call 'repay()'; failing to verify the ending pool balance allows an attacker to borrow 10,000 ETH and simply walk away with the funds. tryIt Friction: Implement an EIP-3156 Flash Loan Provider in Solidity 0.8.20: deploy a liquidity pool; implement flashLoan(receiver, token, amount, data); execute receiver callback; assert ending balance &ge; initial balance + fee; write an Arbitrage Receiver contract that borrows 100 ETH, trades across two AMM pools with a 2% price discrepancy, repays the loan, and pockets 1.8 ETH profit in a single atomic transaction. 🪜 3-Tier Socratic Hint Ladder: [1. Production arbitrage bots exclusively route transactions through private MEV relays like Flashbots.
