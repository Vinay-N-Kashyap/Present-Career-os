Contrast Optimistic Rollups (rely on game-theoretic dispute periods: wait 7 days) with ZK-Rollups (rely on pure mathematics: Validity Proofs). Understand how ZK-Rollups work: an off-chain prover generates a succinct cryptographic proof (ZK-SNARK or ZK-STARK) proving that 10,000 transactions were executed correctly conforming to EVM rules; an on-chain verifier smart contract verifies the proof on Layer 1 in milliseconds for ~250,000 gas! As soon as the proof is verified on L1, transactions achieve Immediate Mathematical Finality with ZERO 7-day delay. Analyze Vitalik's zkEVM Taxonomy: Type 1 (fully Ethereum-equivalent) to Type 4 (high-level language equivalent).
