# B.Com Taxation - pre-screen of checklist (as of 2026-11-26)

Pre-screen only, before the CA review. 100 items: OK 67 / WRONG 9 / UNSURE 24.

## Likely wrong

1. **Day 3, Part 3** - "double tax exposure across US (25%) and UK (15%)".
   Correct: US federal corporate rate is 21% (IRC s.11) and UK main corporation tax rate is 25% (since 1 Apr 2023). The two figures look swapped. If these are meant as blended or illustrative rates, the lesson should say so.
   Source: IRC s.11; gov.uk Corporation Tax rates.

2. **Day 14, Part 1** - Share buybacks "under Section 115QA versus the landmark paradigm shift introduced by Finance Act 2024".
   Correct: the deemed-dividend treatment (s.2(22)(f)) applied to buybacks from 1 Oct 2024 to 31 Mar 2026. Budget 2026 and the Finance Act 2026 moved buyback proceeds back to capital gains in the shareholder's hands. This applies under the Income-tax Act 2025 (in force from 1 Apr 2026), with an extra tax on promoters (reported as about 22% for corporate promoters and 30% for others). The lesson presents the 2024 regime as current, so it is outdated. Check the exact section numbers and rates in the enacted text.
   Source: https://taxguru.in/income-tax/budget-2026-buyback-taxation-capital-gains-treatment-shareholders-additional-tax-promoters.html ; https://www.businesstoday.in/markets/story/buyback-tax-how-india-changed-the-rules-three-times-in-18-months-and-what-investors-need-to-know-553004-2026-09-03

3. **Day 14, Part 5** - "Finance Act 2024 amended s.2(22) by inserting sub-clause (f)... Section 46A allows the acquisition cost as a capital loss."
   Correct: historically accurate for 1 Oct 2024 to 31 Mar 2026. As of 2026 the buyback reference is removed from the dividend definition, and buyback consideration is taxed as capital gains with cost deducted in computing the gain. No separate capital loss arises. Same sources as item 2 (the Elplaw Budget Buzz note says cost is deducted in computing the gain).

4. **Day 14, Part 5** - "company pays $0 tax, shareholder pays $645.8M dividend tax while receiving a $1,200M capital loss".
   Correct: the arithmetic is illustrative, but the outcome is outdated for buybacks on or after 1 Apr 2026 (capital gains, not dividend, plus the promoter levy). Same sources as item 2.

5. **Day 22, Part 5** - "Finance Act 2021... abolishing Section 153A and bringing all search-related assessments under the streamlined Section 147/148 regime."
   Correct: for searches under s.132 initiated on or after 1 Sep 2024, the Finance (No. 2) Act 2024 reintroduced block assessment (Chapter XIV-B, ss.158BA to 158BI; assessment under s.158BC for a six-year block period). The "all search cases go to s.147/148" statement is therefore outdated. The terms "specialized block assessment ... 153A-153D" are also loose, because 153A to 153D were not block assessment provisions.
   Source: https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/w/section-158bg-30 ; https://www.taxmann.com/post/blog/analysis-block-assessment-scheme-under-the-finance-no-2-act ; https://www.ey.com/en_in/technical/alerts-hub/2024/08/key-amendments-to-finance-no-2-bill-2024-at-the-enactment-stage

6. **Day 33, Part 5** - "Section 165A... Equalisation Levy 2.0 levies a 2% tax on gross consideration for e-commerce supply".
   Correct: s.165A(4) says it does not apply to consideration for e-commerce supplies made on or after 1 Aug 2024 (Finance (No. 2) Act 2024). The 6% levy on online advertising was withdrawn from 1 Apr 2025 (Finance Act 2025). Both are now historical. The income-tax Act 2025 has no equivalent.
   Source: https://incometaxindia.gov.in/w/section-165a ; https://www.business-standard.com/industry/news/india-equalisation-levy-removed-tax-impact-2025-125040100810_1.html

7. **Day 39, Part 3** - "Section 14A IGST... twenty-eight percent GST on full face value of bets" for online money gaming.
   Correct: the 28% rate applied from 1 Oct 2023 (Notification 11/2023-CT (Rate)). Following the 56th GST Council, the rate for online money gaming, casinos and similar actionable claims became 40% from 22 Sep 2025. The Promotion and Regulation of Online Gaming Act 2025 has also banned online money games, reportedly from 1 May 2026, so the 14A registration framework is largely moot. Confirm the notification number in the CBIC rate notification.
   Source: https://taxguru.in/?p=1081145 ; https://www.latestly.com/socially/india/news/new-gst-rates-40-gst-announced-on-betting-casinos-gambling-horse-racing-lottery-online-money-gaming-and-admission-to-ipl-matches-7092908.html ; https://cbic-gst.gov.in (Notification 11/2023-CT(Rate) and the 2025 successor)

8. **Day 49, Part 3** - s.270AA immunity "from criminal prosecution under Section 276C or Section 276CCC (failure to furnish returns in search cases)".
   Correct: s.270AA(1) covers prosecution under s.276C and s.276CC (wilful failure to furnish a return), not s.276CCC.
   Source: https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/w/section-270aa

9. **Day 55, Part 4** - "Supreme Court in CIT v. P. Mohanakala and the Delhi High Court in CIT v. Softbrands India P. Ltd., comparables... cannot be agitated under Section 260A".
   Correct: Softbrands India (June 2018) is a Karnataka High Court judgment, not Delhi. Its holding that comparables are pure questions of fact was set aside by the Supreme Court in SAP Labs India v. ITO. The High Court can examine whether the Act and Rules were followed in determining the arm's length price, though it still cannot interfere absent perversity. The proposition as stated is outdated.
   Source: https://itatonline.org/archives/?p=18724 ; https://taxmann.com/post/blog/transfer-pricing-litigation-insights-from-the-post-sc-decision-in-sap-labs
